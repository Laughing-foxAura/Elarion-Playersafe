## Overview
Thalor Sylvae is the ancient forest kingdom of the elves. Once active in continental affairs, the elves have largely withdrawn into their ancestral groves, leaving the world to unfold without them. Their kingdom is cloaked in dense, enchanted woodlands—resistant to divination and nearly impossible to enter without permission.

- Type: Dreaming Monarchy  
- Ruler: Queen Liraethe Velyndor 
- Capital: Thandoril, the Glade of Stars  
- Military: Elite rangers and forest guardians  
- Alignment: True Neutral

---

## Culture & Society

`~Elves in Thalor Sylvae live in harmony with their surroundings. Homes are built into tree canopies, songcraft channels magic, and the boundary between dream and reality is fluid. Though aloof, the elves are not cruel—they simply observe a longer timeline.~`

`~- Language: Elvish (Old Tongue), Sylvan  ~`
`~- Religion: Dream of the World-Soul, star spirits, and ancestral memory ~` 
`~- View on Magic: Inherent, flowing through their music, dreams, and rituals~`

---

## The Sleeping Queen

`~!Queen !Liraethe !Velyndor has not awakened in over a century, yet her influence persists. From her bed of woven starlight, she dreams of countless futures and can speak telepathically to those with elven blood or deep attunement to magic.~`

`~- Her caretakers are an all-female order known as the Twilight Chorus.~`
`~- The Chorus interprets her dreams and guides the kingdom in her stead.~`

>`~ Those who have seen her eyes open say they contain constellations, and her voice echoes like divine music.~`

---

## Sacred Orders of Thalor Sylvae

These houses are not political powers, but devoted stewards, bound by oath and ancestry to the queen. They serve specific functions to preserve the culture, magic, and vision of Thalor Sylvae.

### Devoted Houses:
- [[House Velasira]] – `~The Twilight Chorus ; keepers of Queen Liraethe’s rest and the Starlight Chamber.~`
- [[House Ennarion]] – `~The Thornbound; border guardians and spirit-bound wardens of the forest.~`
- [[House Saelthir]] – `~The Songkeepers; dream-lore bards and chroniclers of the queen’s vision.~`
- [[House Yriveth]] – The Hollow Veil; elusive diplomats and maintainers of elven illusion.
- [[House Elenrath]] – `~The Rootweavers; leyline stewards and wildshape mystics attuned to her dreaming soul.~`

---

## Capital: Thandoril

Located deep in the Dreaming Glade, Thandoril is a luminous forest city woven from light, leaves, and music. The buildings sing in the wind, and the trees whisper when visitors approach.

- Notable Locations:
  `~- The Starroot Throne (Queen’s resting place)~`
  `~- Pool of Unwoven Futures (used in dream magic)~`
  `~- Songweaver Archives (elven histories and prophecies)~`
