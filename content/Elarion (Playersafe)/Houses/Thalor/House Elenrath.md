## `~The Rootweavers~`

> `~“What she dreams, we grow.”~`

---

## Overview

`~House Elenrath are stewards of the land’s heartbeat — wildshapers, ley-gardeners, and caretakers of the sacred groves. They do not command nature, they commune with it. Where their hands touch, the forest bends, grows, and heals.~`

`~They believe the **very forest is a living extension of Queen Liraethe Velyndor’s dreaming soul**, and thus treat their labor as divine communion.~`

---

## Role in Thalor Sylvae

`~- Shape and tend to living pathways, sanctuaries, and hidden glades~`
`~- Safeguard **leyline flows** and remove creeping corruption or imbalance~`
`~- Train elven youth~`

---

## Traditions & Beliefs

`~- Rootweavers do not use tools — they ask, and the forest provides ~`
`~- Their spells are subtle, blooming with scent and warmth rather than light and noise ~`
`~- The **Verdant Rites** are seasonal ceremonies where new growth is woven into prophetic patterns~`
