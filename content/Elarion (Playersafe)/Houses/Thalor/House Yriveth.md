## The Hollow Veil

> “The world must never forget us—nor truly find us.”

---

## Overview

House Yriveth serves as the `~shadowed face~` of [[Thalor Sylvae]], tasked with managing all elven interactions beyond the forest's veil. They are `~illusionists, !diplomats, spies, and stewards of silence.~` When other kingdoms think they’ve spoken to “the elves,” they’ve spoken to Yriveth.

Even among other sacred houses, Yriveth members are regarded with caution. They preserve the queen’s `~secrecy not by !concealment alone — but by curating what !others believe is !truth.~`

---

## Role in Thalor Sylvae

- Serve as **emissaries** to foreign courts, rarely revealing their true identities  
- `~Weave illusions and **veilcraft** to obscure the forest’s true borders and population~`  
- `~Maintain false trails, false legends, and false witnesses in distant lands~`

---

## Traditions & Beliefs

- `~Every member renounces their birth name, adopting a title chosen in ritual mist~`
- `~**Veiled Reflections**: Rites held under moonless nights, where members cleanse their minds of unwanted truths~`  
- `~They believe **perception is reality**, and reality must sometimes be protected from itself~`
