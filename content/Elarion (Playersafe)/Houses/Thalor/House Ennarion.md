## `~The Thornbound~`

> `~“The roots grow inward. The thorns face out.”~`

---

## Overview

`~House Ennarion protects the **outer edges of Thalor Sylvae**, repelling intruders and scouting threats from beyond the forest. They are elite rangers, archers, and spirit-bound warriors, often the only elves outsiders encounter.~`

`~Their bond with the forest runs deep — some members are partially fused with thorned bark or wield bows grown from ancestor-trees.~`

---

## Role in Thalor Sylvae

`~- Patrol and guard the forest’s edge  ~`
`~- Handle **first contact** with trespassers — sometimes deadly, sometimes diplomatic ~` 
`~- Maintain **spirit wards** that protect the glades from corruption or scrying~`

---

## Traditions & Beliefs

`~- Warriors undergo the **Rootmarking**, a ritual where thorns are bound to their flesh~`  
`~- Those who die in service are buried standing, facing outward, to ward eternally~`  
`~- They believe the **true line of defense** is intention — not just weapons~`
