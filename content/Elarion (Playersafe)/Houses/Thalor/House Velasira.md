##  `~The Twilight Chorus~` 

>`~ “We do not serve a crown, we serve her breath.”~`

---

## Overview

`~House Velasira is a sacred order of women charged with protecting the slumber of Queen Liraethe Velyndor and tending the inner sanctum of the **Starlight Chamber**. Their existence is deeply ritualized, and their loyalty lies with the queen’s spirit more than the forest itself.~`

`~Only females may join. Members wear robes of starlight silk and are trained in both spiritual trancework and silent combat.~`

---

## Role in Thalor Sylvae

`~- Guardians of the **Starlight Chamber**, where the queen dreams  ~`
`~- Keepers of her voice in trance — the **High Moonwarden** echoes the queen’s thoughts aloud  ~`
`~- Maintain sacred silence and attend to the magical rhythms of her breath and dreams~`

---

## Traditions & Beliefs

`~-New initiates undergo the **Moonbinding**, aligning their life’s rhythm with the queen’s sleep~`
`~-They sing **Lunarsongs**, harmonic tones that help stabilize the dreaming veil ~` 
`~- Velasira believe their queen is divine in spirit, and will one day awaken only when the stars align~`
