## `~The Songkeepers~`

> `~“Our history sleeps before it wakes.”~`

---

## Overview

`~House Saelthir preserves the **dream-lore and history** of the elves, particularly the future-visions dreamt by Queen Liraethe Velyndor. They record these possibilities in the form of song, mural, and rune — some of which have not yet come true.~`

`~They are not prophets, but stewards of memory across time.~`

---

## Role in Thalor Sylvae

`~- Weave dreams into **songs, murals, and legends**~`  
`~- Keep the **Book of Slumbering Threads**, an ever-growing tome of potential fates~`  
`~- Interpret overlapping dreams shared by the queen and sensitive elves~`

---

## Traditions & Beliefs

`~- Young Songkeepers are trained to **remember dreams not their own**~`  
`~- They believe **some truths live in melody before they manifest**  ~`
`~- Certain ballads are only performed when their time arrives — never before~`
